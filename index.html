Research

Instability-driven mixing in supersonic flows

Undergraduate thesis, July 2026 – May 2027. Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Fazlar Rahman

  • Building an OpenFOAM framework to study passive fuel–air mixing driven by the Richtmyer–Meshkov instability (RMI) under scramjet-relevant conditions.
  • Varying Mach number and Atwood number to measure interface growth rate, baroclinic vorticity deposition and mixing-layer development.
  • The validated case setup will be released as open source with the thesis.

Sinusoidal heat-shield profiles on the Orion re-entry vehicle

AUST Applied Modelling and Simulation Lab, 2025 – present

  • Density-based RANS (SST k–ω, ANSYS Fluent) at Mach 6 and zero incidence: four sinusoidal heat shields (60 mm amplitude, 1–4 waves) compared with the smooth baseline, extending Juhany et al. (2024).
  • Validated against wind-tunnel drag data and published shock standoff distances.
  • The wavy shields moved the bow shock 29–50% upstream, raised CD by 4–17%, and lowered the shield-averaged heat flux by 7–21% at a 300 K wall.

Rayleigh–Taylor instability

Independent work, 2026

  • Simulated the instability between two fluids of different density under acceleration with OpenFOAM's VOF solvers.
  • Followed the growth of interface perturbations into mushroom-shaped structures and the developing mixing layer; post-processed in ParaView.

Case files: github.com/prithuldas/CFD-Portfolio

Temperature-resolved chilled-water storage in a district cooling system

2025 – present. Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Dewan Hasan Ahmed

  • Rebuilt a published Shanghai district cooling plant in OpenModelica, replacing its variable-level store with a temperature-resolved stratified tank from the Modelica Buildings library.
  • Reproduced the published storage inventory within 0.25% and showed that only about 17% of the 80,000 m³ store takes part in the daily cycle.
